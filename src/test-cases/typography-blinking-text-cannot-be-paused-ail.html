--- title: "Blinking text cannot be paused, stopped or hidden" category: "Typography" origin: ail-2026 corrects: typography-blink-element-found categoryOrder: 4 order: 9 ---

Blinking text can distract the reader's attention. This is especially relevant to people with attention deficits or cognitive disabilities. This text blinks for more than five seconds and there is no mechanism to pause, stop or hide it.
