--- title: "Blink element found" category: "Typography" origin: govuk-2017 categoryOrder: 4 order: 3 --- Blinking text and moving text (such as a marquee) can distract the reader's attention. This is especially relevant to people with attention deficits or cognitive disabilities. Neither is likely to cause a seizure, but they are likely to decrease the readability of the document as a whole and increase the time it takes for users to finish reading it.
