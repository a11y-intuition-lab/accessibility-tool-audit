Recycling one glass bottle saves enough energy to power a light bulb for four hours.

Rinse food and drink containers before you put them in your recycling bin.

Flatten cardboard boxes so that more fits in the bin.

Plastic bags and film cannot be recycled at home. Take them to a supermarket collection point.

Batteries must never go in your bin. Take them to a recycling centre or a shop that sells batteries.

Garden waste can go in the brown bin from March to November.

Large items such as sofas can be collected for a fee.
