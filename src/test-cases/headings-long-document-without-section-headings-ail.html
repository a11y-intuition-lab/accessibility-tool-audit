You can apply for a Blue Badge if you have a condition that affects your mobility, or if you are registered blind.

Some people get a Blue Badge automatically, for example if they get the higher rate of the mobility component of Disability Living Allowance.

Other people can apply if they have difficulty walking, cannot walk at all, or have a hidden disability that makes journeys difficult.

To apply you need a recent digital photo, your National Insurance number and the details of your current Blue Badge if you have one.

You will also need proof of identity, such as a birth certificate or passport, and proof of address, such as a Council Tax bill.

It costs up to £10 in England. You do not pay until your application has been approved.

Applications usually take about 12 weeks. Your council may ask you to have a mobility assessment.

If your application is turned down, you can ask the council to reconsider. You must do this within 28 days.

A Blue Badge lets you park closer to your destination, for example on yellow lines for up to 3 hours.

You must display the badge on the dashboard so that the details can be read through the windscreen.

You can be fined up to £1,000 if you misuse a Blue Badge, for example by letting someone else use it.

Your badge is valid for up to 3 years. Your council will usually write to you when it is time to renew.
